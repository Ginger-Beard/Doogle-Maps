--- Research for compost runs (2026-08-11, wiki-checked) ---

What the plugin ALREADY has, and it is more than expected: PatchRules decodes both bin varbits
in full - EMPTY / FILLING (with the count of items already in) / GROWING / HARVESTABLE, and
which product is in there (compost, supercompost, ultracompost, rotten tomato), for the normal
bin and the big one. FarmingWorldData carries a COMPOST patch in every allotment region and the
BIG_COMPOST in the Farming Guild. So the state half is done; nothing reads it yet, because
PlantingGroups.RUNNABLE leaves the bins out ("no seed at all").

The mechanics a run has to model:
- Capacity 15 items (normal bin) / 30 (big bin), un-noted. Fill, then CLOSE the lid; it takes
  35-70 minutes. Items cannot be taken back out, only dumped.
- All 15/30 supercompostable -> supercompost. All tomatoes -> ROTTEN TOMATOES, which is a trap
  worth warning about rather than silently allowing. Anything else -> ordinary compost.
- Emptying needs empty buckets, one per compost, or a bottomless compost bucket. Ordinary
  buckets fill first when both are carried.
- Upgrades happen IN the bin, before withdrawing, and the bin is much cheaper than doing it per
  bucket:
    compost -> supercompost: 1 dose of compost potion for the whole bin.
    supercompost -> ultracompost: 25 volcanic ash (normal bin) or 50 (big bin). Per bucket it
    is 2 ash each, so 30 for the same 15 - the bin saves 5.
  No Farming level for either.
- Volcanic ash: mined from ash piles at the Fossil Island volcano (22 Mining, 1 ash a drop
  rising to 6 at 97), dropped by Fossil Island monsters, and - the one that matters for a run -
  sold unlimited by Petrified Pete at 40gp. Fossil Island itself needs Bone Voyage.

Supercompostable, from the Supercompost page: pineapple, watermelon, papaya, coconut (and half
coconut), dragonfruit, jangerberries, white berries, poison ivy berries, snape grass, mushroom,
white lily, calquat fruit, celastrus bark, ent branch, tenti pineapple, white tree fruit, the
five tree roots (oak/willow/maple/yew/magic), and the herbs from toadflax up (toadflax, avantoe,
kwuarm, snapdragon, cadantine, lantadyme, dwarf weed, torstol). Wants to be a hand-written table
like NotableHarvests - PatchImplementation is generated and cannot carry it.

Open design question before any of this is written: is a compost run its OWN run type, or are
bin stops folded into every run? Every bin but the guild's sits beside allotment and herb
patches you are already standing at, so a separate trip is mostly a wasted circuit - but the
bank trip is real and different (15 items per bin, plus buckets, plus ash), and that is the part
that wants planning up front rather than discovering at the patch.

--- Compost runs: the shape decided (owner, 2026-08-11) ---

A run type of its own, a tickable line like Herb or Tree, routing the bins.

Fill: a selector populated the way the seed list is - the supercompostable items, from what the
account actually has - so the run knows what it is filling with and the loadout can bank 15 (or
30 for the guild's big bin) of it per bin.

Volcanic ash: a checkbox per the protection-payment control, NOT a fill choice. It applies to a
bin that is already ready, and the ORDER is the part that matters and is easy to get wrong:
ash goes in AFTER the bin is ready to harvest and BEFORE any bucket is filled - 25 for a normal
bin, 50 for the big one, upgrading the whole bin at once. Once a bucket is filled it is 2 ash
each and the saving is gone.

Buckets go through the leprechaun, both ways: empty buckets fetched from his store, and the
filled compost buckets of whatever tier deposited straight back to him rather than carried.
This needs nothing new to read - FarmingTool already has EMPTY_BUCKET ("where the empties go
back") and the three compost tiers, and LeprechaunStore reads all four counts every tick.

So the step order at a bin: apply ash if ticked and it is supercompost -> take empty buckets
from the leprechaun -> empty the bin -> deposit the filled buckets with him -> fill with the
chosen item -> close the lid.
