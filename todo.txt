--- Research for compost runs (2026-08-11, wiki-checked) ---

What the plugin ALREADY has, and it is more than expected: PatchRules decodes both bin varbits
in full - EMPTY / FILLING (with the count of items already in) / GROWING / HARVESTABLE, and
which product is in there (compost, supercompost, ultracompost, rotten tomato), for the normal
bin and the big one. FarmingWorldData carries a COMPOST patch in every allotment region and the
BIG_COMPOST in the Farming Guild. So the state half is done; nothing reads it yet, because
PlantingGroups.RUNNABLE leaves the bins out ("no seed at all").

The mechanics a run has to model:
- Capacity 15 items (normal bin) / 30 (big bin), un-noted. Fill, then CLOSE the lid; it takes
  35-70 minutes. Items cannot be taken back out, only dumped.
- All 15/30 supercompostable -> supercompost. All tomatoes -> ROTTEN TOMATOES, which is a trap
  worth warning about rather than silently allowing. Anything else -> ordinary compost.
- Emptying needs empty buckets, one per compost, or a bottomless compost bucket. Ordinary
  buckets fill first when both are carried.
- Upgrades happen IN the bin, before withdrawing, and the bin is much cheaper than doing it per
  bucket:
    compost -> supercompost: 1 dose of compost potion for the whole bin.
    supercompost -> ultracompost: 25 volcanic ash (normal bin) or 50 (big bin). Per bucket it
    is 2 ash each, so 30 for the same 15 - the bin saves 5.
  No Farming level for either.
- Volcanic ash: mined from ash piles at the Fossil Island volcano (22 Mining, 1 ash a drop
  rising to 6 at 97), dropped by Fossil Island monsters, and - the one that matters for a run -
  sold unlimited by Petrified Pete at 40gp. Fossil Island itself needs Bone Voyage.

Supercompostable, from the Supercompost page: pineapple, watermelon, papaya, coconut (and half
coconut), dragonfruit, jangerberries, white berries, poison ivy berries, snape grass, mushroom,
white lily, calquat fruit, celastrus bark, ent branch, tenti pineapple, white tree fruit, the
five tree roots (oak/willow/maple/yew/magic), and the herbs from toadflax up (toadflax, avantoe,
kwuarm, snapdragon, cadantine, lantadyme, dwarf weed, torstol). Wants to be a hand-written table
like NotableHarvests - PatchImplementation is generated and cannot carry it.

Open design question before any of this is written: is a compost run its OWN run type, or are
bin stops folded into every run? Every bin but the guild's sits beside allotment and herb
patches you are already standing at, so a separate trip is mostly a wasted circuit - but the
bank trip is real and different (15 items per bin, plus buckets, plus ash), and that is the part
that wants planning up front rather than discovering at the patch.

--- Compost runs: the shape decided (owner, 2026-08-11) ---

A run type of its own, a tickable line like Herb or Tree, routing the bins.

Fill: a selector populated the way the seed list is - the supercompostable items, from what the
account actually has - so the run knows what it is filling with and the loadout can bank 15 (or
30 for the guild's big bin) of it per bin.

Volcanic ash: a checkbox per the protection-payment control, NOT a fill choice. It applies to a
bin that is already ready, and the ORDER is the part that matters and is easy to get wrong:
ash goes in AFTER the bin is ready to harvest and BEFORE any bucket is filled - 25 for a normal
bin, 50 for the big one, upgrading the whole bin at once. Once a bucket is filled it is 2 ash
each and the saving is gone.

Buckets go through the leprechaun, both ways: empty buckets fetched from his store, and the
filled compost buckets of whatever tier deposited straight back to him rather than carried.
This needs nothing new to read - FarmingTool already has EMPTY_BUCKET ("where the empties go
back") and the three compost tiers, and LeprechaunStore reads all four counts every tick.

So the step order at a bin: apply ash if ticked and it is supercompost -> take empty buckets
from the leprechaun -> empty the bin -> deposit the filled buckets with him -> fill with the
chosen item -> close the lid.

--- Compost runs: what is built, and what is next (2026-08-11) ---

DONE, with tests (CompostablesTest):
- Compostables - the supercompostable table, wiki-scraped, insertion-ordered for a selector.
  Grimy herbs are in and their gameval names are UNIDENTIFIED_*, not anything with "grimy".
  White lily is deliberately absent: the flower has no harvested item, so there is nothing to
  put in a bin. The tomato trap is a method of its own.
- CompostBin - the per-size arithmetic: 15/30 capacity, 25/50 ash, one compost potion dose for
  either size, and the bucket and ash item ids. The test pins ash-in-the-bin as cheaper than
  two-per-bucket, which is the fact the step order exists to protect.

ALL BUILT (2026-08-11, second pass) - the list below was the plan and every line landed:
1. A fill selection store, keyed like SeedSelectionStore, over Compostables.superCompostables()
   filtered by what the bank actually holds.
2. An ash checkbox alongside the protection controls (ProtectionSelectionStore is the shape to
   copy - it is already a per-group boolean the panel renders).
3. Loadout rows: capacity x bins of the chosen fill, ash x bins when ticked, and empty buckets -
   which come from the leprechaun, so they are AT_LEPRECHAUN rather than a withdrawal whenever
   his store has them (LeprechaunStore already reads EMPTY_BUCKET and the three compost tiers).
4. The run option itself. NOT via PlantingGroups.RUNNABLE: that path assumes a seed, and
   Seed.forPatchType(COMPOST) is empty, so a bin would break the seed selector and the
   harvest-only derivation. It wants a RunOption whose group is a bin rather than a planting
   group, which is the one structural change in the whole feature.
5. Guide steps at a bin, in the order the owner specified: ash if ticked and it is supercompost
   -> empty buckets from the leprechaun -> empty the bin -> deposit the filled buckets with him
   -> fill with the chosen item -> close the lid. PatchRules already decodes every state this
   needs, including how many items are in a part-filled bin.

--- Compost runs: BUILT (2026-08-11) ---

The whole feature is in, tests green (830 tests):
- CompostRunStore: fill choice + ash toggle, per RS profile, validated against Compostables in
  both directions so a stale id can never fill bins with the wrong thing. Deliberately not in
  ProfileReset - both values are choices, which the reset's doctrine keeps.
- Run option: one "Compost bin" line pinned before the contract (PlantingGroups.addBinRun); the
  tick widens to the guild's big bin in RunTypeStore.getSelected, the same fold the tab makes.
  RunPlanner.inTheRun folds bins onto that line; binActionable routes to bins that are empty
  or collectable (owner's call) - only a closed bin still composting is skipped, until the
  clock says it must be done.
- Loadout (RunLoadout.addCompostBinSupplies off RunPlanner.binWork): fill x remaining capacity
  (un-noted, with the pack-space warning), ash x 25/50 for ready SUPERCOMPOST bins when ticked,
  and the carriers - bottomless bucket outright when owned, otherwise empties AT_LEPRECHAUN.
- Guide (CompostBinPlan, branched from GuideTracker.stepsFor before any seed machinery): reads
  the SNAPSHOT, not the projection - the projection flattens both bin counts (FILLING count not
  carried, HARVESTABLE count only as livesRemaining). Order is the owner's: APPLY_ASH first
  while everything is still in the bin, then withdraw empties, EMPTY_BIN, DEPOSIT_COMPOST
  (full buckets to the lep, before the fill needs the slots), FILL_BIN, CLOSE_BIN. Bottomless
  bucket short-circuits the whole dance. Rotten tomatoes come out by hand with a "this fill
  made no compost" step. New GuideActions ride the existing leprechaun two-pane machinery
  (DEPOSIT_COMPOST joins RETURN_BUCKETS on your side of his store).
- UI: CompostBinPanel on the compost tab's footer (the slot the seed selector fills elsewhere) -
  owned supercompostables as bank-style icons, click to pick with the seed list's green tint,
  ash checkbox with the owned count in its tooltip.
- Tests: CompostRunStoreTest, CompostBinPlanTest (step order incl. ash-before-buckets and the
  tomato trap), RunPlannerTest.binsRouteWhenEmptyOrCollectable, six RunLoadoutTest rows.

Not modelled, on purpose: compost potions (only matter for a bin filled with junk, which the
fill validation prevents); mid-run partial-empty pack juggling beyond the re-derive (the pure-
function step model already handles it - fewer buckets just means another round).


--- Seaweed and coral runs: BUILT (2026-08-11, same pass) ---

Research first, and the ground truth turned out to be in RuneLite core's own menu-option
comments (the decode's provenance strings), which beat the wiki for mechanics:
- Seaweed is a CONVENTIONAL patch underwater: "Seaweed patch[Rake]", "Seaweed[Pick]",
  "Diseased seaweed[Cure]" (plant cure), "Dead seaweed[Clear]". Every generic guide branch
  was already right; seaweed needed NOTHING beyond what the RUNNABLE addition gave it.
- The coral nursery is NOT: its empty state (varbit 0-3) decodes as fully-weedy WEEDS but the
  menu is "Coral nursery[Inspect,Guide]" - no Rake option at all; grown coral (8/19/30) is
  "[Collect]" with no HARVESTABLE decode (the projection's promotion covers routing); diseased
  coral is "[Prune]" - secateurs, not a plant cure; a frag is PLACED, no dibber; no compost
  (already handled); "a spade is not required to harvest".

What changed:
- GuidePlan: CORAL joins GRAPES in the rake exclusion; CORAL joins PRUNED_HEALTHY; the sow
  branch skips the dibber for frags; "Place the X frag on the nursery" / "Collect the X from
  the nursery" wording. Plus one GLOBAL guard the nursery exposed: the harvest branch now
  refuses WEEDS outright - the projection promotes a fully-weedy patch to HARVESTABLE, the
  rake branch used to mask it, and coral (rake-exempt) fell through to "harvest the weeds".
- ToolNeeds: requiredFor drops bin types first (a bin-only run was told to carry a rake and
  spade for two lidded boxes - live wart from the compost work, fixed here); the rake needs a
  rakeable type on the run (GRAPES/CORAL never are); anythingWeedy exempts CORAL like GRAPES;
  plantsAnySeed skips frags; secateurs added for CORAL runs (prune is the only cure there).
- TeleportItems: Digsite pendant (all five charges) -> Seaweed region 15008, and in the
  defaults. The coral nurseries get no row on purpose: the route is Sailing (charter to
  Deepfin Point + rowboat), which no bankable item the table could name performs.
- Diving gear/medallion loadout rows were already right from the earlier pass; DiseaseRisk
  already knows both are protectable (Mernia 200 numulite / Chet 5-of-the-tier-below);
  coral's no-compost was already in CropYieldModel.TAKES_NO_COMPOST.
Tests: GuidePlanTest x5 (placement-not-rake, prune, Collect, seaweed keeps its rake, dead
coral clears), RunLoadoutTest x2 (coral tool list, bin-only run needs no ground tools).

--- The last runnable patch types: researched, three built, two rejected (2026-08-11) ---

Researched whether people actually RUN each of the five that were left:
- GRAPES - yes, genuinely. Twelve patches in one place (Hosidius Vinery), 35-minute growth,
  level 36, seeds from Tithe Farm, farmed for Zamorak/Saradomin wines. A real circuit.
- ANIMA - one Farming Guild patch that produces NOTHING (100 xp on planting, no check, no
  harvest). Its value is the global buff on every other run: attas +5% harvest saves, iasor
  -80% disease, kronos stage skips. Dies after ~3.5 days and must be replanted.
- CRYSTAL_TREE - one patch, Prifddinas, level 74 (NOT 94; several third-party guides say 94
  and are wrong). 8h growth, chopped with an axe, 13,240 check xp, 28-32 shards with ultra.
  Cannot be diseased. Harvested and removed together - no stump, patch empty immediately.
- REDWOOD - NO. Plant once at 90; after that you chop it for logs and it REGROWS by itself.
  Only ever revisited if disease kills it.
- SPIRIT_TREE - NO. A grown one is a permanent teleport hub, never harvested. Planting is the
  entire interaction.
- HESPORI - skipped by the owner: it grows a demi-boss to fight, not a crop to service.

Built (grapes as a full run, the other two as single-patch lines that route when the patch
wants something - RunPlanner's generic isReady/isEmpty path already covered both):
- crop-xp rows: grape 31.5/625/40, crystal acorn 126/13240/-, attas+iasor+kronos 100/-/-.
  "Crystal acorn" needed an XP_NAME_OVERRIDES entry (its Seed constant is CRYSTAL_TREE).
- GRAPES/CRYSTAL_TREE/ANIMA into PlantingGroups.RUNNABLE, with the exclusions written down.
- ToolNeeds: a GARDENING_TROWEL for the vinery. The loadout already banked its saltpetre and
  nothing to spread it with - no other family prepares its soil, so nothing had asked before.
- PatchProjection.isChoppable + RunLoadout.NEEDS_AN_AXE take CRYSTAL_TREE: its harvest IS the
  chop, so an axeless trip collects no shards. Worded "the patch is left empty" because there
  is genuinely no stump to come back for.
- GuidePlan 0.8, the real gap: a spent ANIMA plant reached NO branch. Every anima state
  decodes as GROWING (core walks "Attas plant" -> "Withering" -> "Dead Attas plant[Clear]"
  without the crop state moving), so the dead one missed the DEAD branch, missed the harvest,
  and fell into growing-leave-it-alone. The run routed there and the guide said nothing. Now
  the last stage gets a spade step; a withering plant is still working and is left alone.
Tests: GuidePlanTest x3 (spent anima dug up, live anima left alone, crystal chopped for
shards), RunLoadoutTest x2 (vinery trowel, and no other family asking for one).

--- Bin fill: both tiers, two collapsible sections (2026-08-11) ---

Only the supercompostable list was offered, which quietly assumed everyone bins pineapples.
Ordinary compost is what most of a farm run actually gets treated with, and an account with no
fruit still has potatoes and grimy guams.

- Compostables gained ordinaryCompostables() and isCompostable() alongside the super list,
  scraped from the same wiki "Suitable items" table: allotment crops, herbs BELOW toadflax
  (clean and grimy), hops and flax, flowers, the bush berries under jangerberry, fruit-tree
  fruit, Forestry leaves, and the odds and ends. Two entries surprise people and are called
  out in the javadoc: GIANT SEAWEED and POTATO CACTUS are ordinary, not super. Deliberately
  not exhaustive at the silly end - kebabs, apple mush and rotten apples are on the wiki's
  table and nobody stockpiles them to compost.
- CompostRunStore now validates against BOTH tables rather than the super one, and gained
  fillMakesSupercompost() so the panel can say which tier a pick yields. The tomato is stored
  rather than refused: the wiki's own escape is "at least one compostable item must be of a
  different type", so only an ENTIRELY tomato bin is wasted and the panel warns instead.
- CompostBinPanel is two collapsible sections - "Supercompost (n)" and "Compost (n)" - each
  folding on its own through PanelLayoutStore, with the owned count on the heading so a folded
  section still says what is in it. The ash tooltip now explains itself when the chosen fill
  makes ordinary compost, since ash upgrades supercompost and nothing else.
Tests: CompostablesTest (cut-off both ways, the two surprises, no overlap between the lists),
CompostRunStoreTest (either tier storable and reported, tomato flagged not refused, a scimitar
refused).
